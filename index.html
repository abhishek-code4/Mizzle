How I got here

⚡

The Sneeze

One grumpy afternoon, a huge thundercloud sneezed over the hill town of Puddlewick. Ah… ah… ACHOO! Out puffed one very small cloud. That was me.

💧

Plink

I tried to rain on the whole town. I managed one drop. Plink. It landed right in the baker's flour, and she laughed, kindly. A cute little disaster.

🌷

The Tulip Shop

Mrs. Tulip, who sells umbrellas, whispered: “Small weather matters. One drop is exactly enough for one thirsty seed.” I have worked that way ever since.

🌈

Now

Today I weave tiny weather at the Weather Workshop: lullaby rain, puddle postcards, umbrella routes. Next stop: wherever you are. Thanks for visiting!
